About Us

KIET is a private university in Karachi, recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan.

It offers BS, MS and MBA programs in computing, artificial intelligence and management sciences. Students also enjoy transport service, sports grounds and an online Learning Management System (LMS).
